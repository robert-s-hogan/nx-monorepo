    Quick reference

    Pull loop

    ▶

    1–15 · two-hander, Battle Stance

    1. Charge, then Rend.
    2. Thunder Clap when 2+ mobs.
    3. Heroic Strike on spare rage. Skip it on a nearly dead mob.
    4. Overpower whenever it lights up.

    16+ · shield, AoE tanking

    1. Bank rage from the last pull.
    2. Bloodrage on the run-in.
    3. Charge. Straight into Defensive once you have Vanguard (29).
    4. Thunder Clap, Battle Shout. Demo Shout after you have threat.
    5. Shield Block up. Revenge on every proc.
    6. Rend mobs that will live. Sunder the ones pulling away.
    7. Spam Cleave and Thunder Clap. Heroic Strike on spare rage.
    8. War Stomp at 3+ mobs or to stop a caster.
    9. Going bad: Last Stand, Retaliation, potion.

    Professions, camp & Legacy

    ▶

    Professions

    • First Aid on every warrior.
    • Herbalism + Skinning for gold. No overlap with your partner's mining.
    • Cooking: food buffs plus bonus kill XP.

    Camping

    • Every profession unlocks a camp item at skill 20. Needs a campfire; one-hour shared cooldown.
    • Skinning: Camp Chair, 2% crit for everyone at the camp.
    • Field Guide upgrade (Skinning 140): blueprint from Lady Anacondra, Wailing Caverns. Adds Track Beasts.

    Legacy points

    • Start at 0. First point: Novice Warrior challenge at 25.
    • Crafting skill 150, 225 and 300 each earn one. Gathering doesn't count.
    • Spend on Thrill of Adventure to 5/5.

    Tauren racials

    ▶
    • Endurance: +5% health, +2% crit damage and healing.
    • War Stomp: 2-second stun on up to 5 nearby enemies. 90-second cooldown.
